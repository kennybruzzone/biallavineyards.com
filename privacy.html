Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

At Bialla Vineyards, we recognize and respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect information through our website at https://biallavineyards.com (the “Site”). All references to ‘our’, ‘us’, ‘we’ or ‘company’ within this policy are deemed to refer to Bialla Vineyards, its subsidiaries, affiliates and associates.

The Site is informational. It does not offer online ordering, customer accounts, payments, a mailing-list sign-up, or public forums. The only personal information we ask for is what you choose to send us through the contact form.

Information We Collect

Information you provide

If you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address and message that you enter. If you call or email us directly, we receive the information you choose to share in that call or email, such as your phone number or email address.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the Site is served by a hosting provider whose servers automatically record basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the date and time of the request. This information is used for security and to operate the Site. We do not use it to identify you.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you send us to read and respond to your inquiry and to keep a record of our correspondence. When you send email or other communication to us, we may retain those communications in order to process your inquiries, respond to your requests and improve our service. We will not add you to a marketing list because you used the contact form.

How We Share Your Information

We understand the sensitivity of giving out personal information. We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with any third-party marketing or sales organizations of any kind, except in the performance of services on behalf of Bialla Vineyards, and with their authorized use limited to that purpose.

Specifically, messages sent through the contact form are delivered to us by our form-processing provider and our email provider, which handle them on our behalf. Our website hosting provider processes the technical information described above. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

Cookies and Tracking

The Site does not use advertising cookies, and we do not use the Site to build advertising profiles. We do not knowingly allow third parties to collect personally identifiable information about your online activities over time and across different websites through the Site.

Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a “Do Not Track” setting. Because there is no common industry standard for how to respond to these signals, the Site does not respond to them. As noted above, we do not track visitors across websites for advertising in any case.

Security and Retention

We take appropriate security measures to protect against the unauthorized access or use, alteration or loss of the information we receive from you. We keep inquiry emails only as long as reasonably needed to respond and for ordinary business records. No method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your California Privacy Rights

We post this policy in accordance with the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA). Under California Civil Code Section 1798.83, California residents may request information about any disclosure of personal information to third parties for their direct marketing purposes. We do not share personal information with third parties for their direct marketing purposes. You may also ask us to access, correct or delete the information you sent us through the Site by contacting us as described below, and California residents may have additional rights under applicable California law.

Child Privacy

Because of the nature of our business, our products are not designed to appeal to minors. The Site is intended only for adults of legal drinking age (21 years of age or older), and we do not knowingly attempt to solicit or receive any information from anyone under 21. If we learn that information has been provided by a person under the legal drinking age, we will delete it.

Linked Sites and Other Parties

This Site may contain links to other websites. This Privacy Policy only addresses our use and disclosure of personal information collected on our Site. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any third parties or the content of linked sites, and we encourage you to read the applicable privacy policies and terms and conditions of such parties or websites.

Changes to This Policy

Please note that this Privacy Policy may change from time to time. We will not reduce your rights under this Privacy Policy without your consent. We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date above.

If you feel the Site is not following this Privacy Policy, or you have any questions about it, please let us know using the contact details below.
